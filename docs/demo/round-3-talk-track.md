## 2. Judge Q&A Defense Cheat Sheet

### Q1: "Isn't macOS market share in India tiny (~2.6%)? How can this be a viable startup?"
* **Answer:** *"Exactly—macOS is our proving ground, not our ceiling. macOS provides the lowest-friction, highest-fidelity accessibility tree to prove our shared-control architecture. Our core engine (`ClickyCore`) is 60% OS-agnostic. Our scaling milestone is strictly gated: at 100 paid macOS seats and 2 enterprise design partners, we launch our Windows UI Automation adapter, which immediately unlocks the 82% of Indian enterprise PCs. Furthermore, global English Mac prosumers at $99/year provide a high-margin USD cash stabilizer."*

### Q2: "What if Google adds desktop tool calling or Apple releases Siri with screen actions?"
* **Answer:** *"The model is a supplier, not the product. If Gemini or Apple improves their speech models, Clicky gets faster and cheaper for free. Our defensibility is three-fold: First, **Enterprise Trust**—companies will never let raw cloud models execute unsanctioned OS clicks without our client-side 5-tier risk gate and local <150ms kill switch. Second, our **Adapter Corpus** solves uninstrumented enterprise apps like SAP and Electron. Third, Apple will never build local distribution for Pune college Equal Opportunity Cells or Indian CSR compliance."*

### Q3: "How do you prevent high Gemini Live token costs from bankrupting the startup?"
* **Answer:** *"We don't stream video or audio blindly. Our Tri-Tier execution engine flattens native windows via the Accessibility tree in under 20 milliseconds at **zero token cost**. Over 90% of user actions require zero vision tokens. On the audio side, Live voice runs at ~₹1 to ₹2 per minute; with our 600 min/mo fair-use cap on consumer tiers and pooled minutes on enterprise licenses, we lock in 50%+ gross margins from day one."*

### Q4: "Why would an HR manager pay ₹49,999 for this instead of waiting for free OS features?"
* **Answer:** *"Because waiting violates current compliance laws. Under SEBI's circular extending to October 2026 and UGC's September 2026 directives, institutions are legally required to provide accessible workplace accommodations today. Over 155 organizations were penalized in early 2025 under the RPwD Act. Our ₹49,999 pilot sits under manager discretionary approval limits, solving their compliance headache immediately with zero tender delays."*
